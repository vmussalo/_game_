# GRISTLE & GLITTER — design bible

Isometric, turn-based, dialogue-heavy wasteland RPG. Original setting: a **biopunk frontier**
where meat grows back, and the only institutions that survived the collapse are **travelling carnivals**.
Tone: semi-grotesque, dry black humour, K16 (body horror and crude jokes, no gore porn).

---

## 1. Premise

Forty-one years ago Abundance Biologics released **PERPETUA**, livestock tissue that regrows whatever is cut
from it. It was meant to end hunger. It did, for about eight months. Then the tissue began to merge, spread
and *bloom* across the plains. Cities choked under the Overgrowth; the government drowned in it.

The plains are now **the Rind**. People live by herding, cutting and eating the flesh. Every ranch town sits
beside a **Mother Herd**, a vast, ancient, sleepy mound of meat that is cut daily and regrows by night.

The only organisations that came through intact were the **carnivals**: they had wagons, generators,
performers, and the habit of leaving town before things got ugly. Today they are mobile city-states called
**Circuits**. Their paper tickets are the currency of the Rind.

## 2. Factions

| Faction | What they want | Flavour |
|---|---|---|
| **The Herd Compact** | Keep the Mother Herds alive and the towns fed | Ranchers, cutters, stubborn matriarchs |
| **The Grand Calliope** | Own the Bloom itself and make it perform | The largest Circuit: a mile-long caravan run by the Ringmistress |
| **The Marrow Show** | Profit. Drain Mother Herds and sell the marrow | A splinter troupe of greasepaint thugs and grafted freaks |
| **The Hide-Takers** | Take what they can, wear what they take | Raiders in tanned Lump-hide |
| **The Sawbones Guild** | Sell "improvements": grafts, extra organs, mutations | Surgeon-merchants, the mutation shop of the Rind |
| **The Quiet Choir** | Be absorbed into the Bloom; the flesh hums to them | Serene, pale, unsettlingly kind |
| **The Shareholders** | Hold the quarterly meeting (it has lasted 41 years) | Abundance executives preserved in nutrient vats |

## 3. Structure (full game)

- **Act 1 – The Wasting** (the vertical slice): Gutwater's Mother Herd is dying. The trail leads to the
  Marrow Show, and its ledger names a buyer: the Grand Calliope.
- **Act 2 – The Circuit**: join, infiltrate or rob the Grand Calliope as it crosses the Rind. The Calliope is a
  *moving location* on the world map. Uncover the *Perpetua Seed*: a plan to start a second Bloom that only the
  Calliope controls.
- **Act 3 – The Last Summer Fair**: the Grand Finale at the Wellspring (the ruins of Abundance HQ). Endings:
  burn the Bloom, crown the Calliope, give the Rind to the Choir, or knit the Mother Herds into one living
  network that no one owns.

Every town gets its own ending slide. Major quests have at least three solutions: violent, talky, sneaky
or technical.

## 4. Planned locations (≈18)

## 5. Character system

**Six attributes (1–10):** Grit, Nerve, Wits, Charm, Reflex, Gut.
Gut matters: it decides what you can eat, drink, smell and survive.

**14 skills:** Guns, Melee, Brawling, Throwing, Doctoring, Butchery, Sneak, Lockwork, Tinkering, Gab, Haggle,
Wrangling, Showmanship, Survival. Three are chosen as **Knacks** (+20, double growth).

**Quirks** (pick up to two at creation) are a double-edged background trait.
**Scars** (one every 3 levels) are permanent perks.
**Mutations** (Act 2+, via the Sawbones) give a bonus and a social cost, and some of them talk.

## 6. Combat

Hex grid, action points, initiative by Sequence. Aimed shots: head, eyes, torso, arms, legs, groin, and
extra parts on mutants (a Gristling's tumour bursts). Critical hits carry grotesque flavour text.
Enemies can flee, beg, or bribe. Companions fight under simple AI.

## 7. Currency and economy

**Tickets** (carnival scrip) are the currency. Haggle and Charm set prices. Carnival towns pay more for oddities;
ranch towns pay more for meat and medicine.

## 8. Vertical slice scope (Act 1, part 1)

- Gutwater (town + pasture + Old Mother), the Sump, a world map with 3 visitable nodes plus teaser nodes, random encounters.
- Main quest with 4 resolutions, 5 side quests, 1 companion (Moe & Lester, the Two-Headed Tenor).
- Character creation, levelling, Scars, inventory, barter, save/load, ending slides.
